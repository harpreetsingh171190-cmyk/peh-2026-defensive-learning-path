# Code of Conduct

We want a respectful, harassment-free community for learners and practitioners.

## Expected behavior
- Be kind, patient and constructive; assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality, including other people's lab notes.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or doxxing.
- Sharing exploit code or data aimed at real third-party targets.
- Pressuring others to test systems without authorization.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and restrict participation, when conduct
violates these rules. Report concerns through the channel in `SECURITY.md`.

Adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

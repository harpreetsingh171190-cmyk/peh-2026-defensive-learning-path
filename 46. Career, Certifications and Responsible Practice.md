# 46. Career, Certifications and Responsible Practice

## Build proof of skill
A good beginner portfolio can contain:
- A threat model for a toy app.
- A sanitized security finding with remediation.
- A parser for synthetic logs with tests.
- A local-only security-header audit.
- A secure configuration checklist.
- A short reflection describing limitations and what you learned.

## Interview framework
For each project, explain the goal, authorized scope, setup, method, evidence, impact, remediation, regression test, and limitations.

## Certification note
Check current official exam objectives, prices, prerequisites, and renewal rules before planning. Course completion is not the same as a certification. Avoid claims such as “certified” unless the credential was actually awarded.

## Professional conduct
Do not access, scan, exploit, or disclose data from systems without explicit authorization. Report findings through the approved channel and follow coordinated disclosure requirements.

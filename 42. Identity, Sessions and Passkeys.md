# 42. Identity, Sessions and Passkeys

## Core concepts
- Authentication proves identity; authorization decides permitted actions.
- Sessions, cookies, refresh tokens, MFA, passkeys/WebAuthn, recovery flows.
- Secure cookie attributes: `Secure`, `HttpOnly`, and an appropriate `SameSite` policy.
- Session rotation after login or privilege changes; revocation on logout and account recovery.
- Rate limiting and abuse monitoring that avoid locking out legitimate users unfairly.

## Lab checklist for an owned demo
- [ ] Test normal login/logout and session invalidation.
- [ ] Verify sensitive actions require server-side authorization.
- [ ] Review account recovery and MFA reset flows.
- [ ] Check cookie flags and transport security.
- [ ] Confirm tokens are not placed in URLs, logs, or analytics.
- [ ] Check that errors do not reveal whether sensitive accounts exist.

Never test credentials or recovery flows against accounts you do not own or have written permission to assess.

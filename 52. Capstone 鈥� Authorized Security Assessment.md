# 52. Capstone — Authorized Security Assessment

## Goal
Demonstrate a complete, bounded assessment of a deliberately local lab or another system for which you have explicit written authorization.

## Required deliverables
1. **Scope:** assets, allowed tests, excluded actions, dates, contact and stop conditions.
2. **Threat model:** assets, trust boundaries, likely threats and assumptions.
3. **Method:** repeatable checks, tools and versions; avoid unnecessary intrusive tests.
4. **Findings:** severity rationale, evidence with secrets redacted, impact and reproduction steps within scope.
5. **Fixes:** specific, prioritized remediation with owners.
6. **Retest:** evidence that fixes worked and note any residual risk.
7. **Reflection:** what was learned, limitations and next steps.

## Quality gate
- No unauthorized public targets.
- No real credential harvesting, persistence, destructive payloads or denial-of-service.
- No secrets or personal data in Git history.
- Every finding has a clear source and a practical fix.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions.
- Include a concise executive summary and a technical appendix.

## Suggested rubric (100 points)
- Scope and safety: 20
- Method reproducibility: 15
- Evidence quality: 20
- Risk prioritization: 15
- Remediation and retest: 20
- Clarity and reflection: 10

A capstone is successful when the owner can understand the risk and verify the fix—not when the report contains the largest number of findings.

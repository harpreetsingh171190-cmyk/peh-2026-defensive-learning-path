# 65. Threat Hunting with Hypothesis-Driven Queries

## Method
1. Write a falsifiable hypothesis (for example: a privileged account was used outside its normal pattern).
2. Identify data sources, time range, expected fields and known coverage gaps.
3. Query authorized logs using read-only access and a bounded time window.
4. Compare normal and suspicious patterns; validate timestamps, identity mapping and parsing.
5. Preserve relevant evidence, minimize personal data and document uncertainty.
6. Escalate according to the incident response plan; do not confront a suspected actor or alter source systems.

## Quality checks
- Are clocks synchronized and time zones explicit?
- Are failed and successful events both represented?
- Could service accounts, NAT, VPN or scheduled jobs explain the behavior?
- Is the hypothesis supported by more than one independent signal?
- Can another analyst reproduce the result?

## Lab
Use synthetic authentication logs in `labs/`. Write one hypothesis, a query or parsing method, two benign explanations, and the evidence needed to distinguish them.

## Deliverable
A short hunt report: hypothesis, scope, data sources, method, results, limitations and next action.

# Module 86 — Resilience Engineering and Recovery Drills

## Goal
Verify that systems recover as designed, using only environments you own.

## Topics
- Recovery objectives: RTO (time to restore) and RPO (acceptable data loss).
- Backup verification: restore to a scratch location and compare hashes (see `labs/integrity_check.py`).
- Dependency mapping: what must come back first?
- Drill design: written scope, rollback plan, stop conditions, observers, and a debrief.

## Lab
Create a folder of fictional files, take a baseline, "lose" some files, restore from your backup, and prove the restore with a verify run. Record the elapsed time and compare it to a target RTO you set.

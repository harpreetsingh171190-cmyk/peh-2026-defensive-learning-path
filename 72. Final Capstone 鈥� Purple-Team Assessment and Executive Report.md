# Module 72 — Final Capstone: Purple-Team Assessment and Executive Report

## Goal
Demonstrate a complete, authorized assessment using a local lab or an explicitly approved training environment. This capstone rewards clear reasoning, defensive value, and safe handling—not the number of tools used.

## Required phases
1. **Authorization:** written scope, dates, permitted methods, contacts, stop conditions.
2. **Threat model:** assets, trust boundaries, likely abuse cases, and assumptions.
3. **Baseline:** versions, configuration, identity controls, and logging.
4. **Testing:** minimal reproducible tests mapped to the agreed scope.
5. **Detection:** identify which logs/alerts should capture each test.
6. **Remediation:** prioritized fixes with owners and target dates.
7. **Verification:** retest the fix and preserve concise evidence.
8. **Reporting:** technical details plus a one-page executive summary.
9. **Retrospective:** what worked, gaps, and next-quarter improvements.

## Scoring rubric (100 points)
- Authorization and safety controls — 15
- Threat model and scope clarity — 10
- Reproducible evidence and note quality — 15
- Technical reasoning and validation — 15
- Detection and logging analysis — 10
- Risk-based remediation plan — 15
- Fix verification and regression tests — 10
- Executive communication and privacy — 10

Suggested completion target: 80/100, with no critical failure in authorization or evidence handling. This is a self-assessment, not an accredited certification.

## Submission checklist
- [ ] Scope and rules of engagement
- [ ] Asset inventory limited to authorized lab assets
- [ ] Threat model
- [ ] Findings register with severity rationale
- [ ] Sanitized evidence references
- [ ] Detection observations
- [ ] Remediation owners and due dates
- [ ] Verification results
- [ ] Executive summary and lessons learned

Never include passwords, tokens, private keys, real personal data, or unauthorized target details in a public portfolio.

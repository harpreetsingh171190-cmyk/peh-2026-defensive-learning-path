# 55. Backup, Recovery and Ransomware Readiness

## Learning goals
- Define recovery point objective (RPO) and recovery time objective (RTO).
- Distinguish synchronization from recoverable backup.
- Practice restore validation safely.

## Checklist
- Identify critical services, owners and data classes.
- Set realistic RPO/RTO targets with service owners.
- Keep at least one backup copy isolated or immutable where feasible.
- Protect backup administration with separate identities and MFA.
- Encrypt backups and control access to keys.
- Monitor backup failures and unusual deletion activity.
- Test restores in an isolated environment on a schedule.
- Record recovery steps, dependencies, contacts and decision authority.
- Preserve evidence and follow incident response procedures if compromise is suspected.

## Tabletop exercise
Scenario: a workstation shows a ransom note. Do not run malware. Discuss who declares an incident, how to isolate affected systems, who preserves evidence, how clean recovery points are selected, and who authorizes restoration.

## Metrics
Backup success rate; restore test success rate; measured restore duration; age of last clean recovery point; unresolved recovery blockers.

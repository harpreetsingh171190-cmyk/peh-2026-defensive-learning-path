# 40. Modern Security Portfolio and Interview Lab

## Portfolio structure
- `README.md`: scope, ethical rules, setup, limitations.
- `threat-model.md`: assets, trust boundaries, prioritized threats.
- `findings/`: sanitized findings with severity and reproducible evidence.
- `detections/`: synthetic log tests and detection rationale.
- `sbom/`: generated inventory with sensitive fields removed.
- `remediation/`: fixes and regression tests.
- `lessons-learned.md`: what changed after validation.

## Interview practice
For each finding, explain:
1. What was the asset and authorized scope?
2. What evidence supports the finding?
3. What are the realistic impact and limitations?
4. What remediation reduces the risk?
5. How would you verify the fix?
6. What telemetry would help detect recurrence?

Never upload real customer data, secrets, tokens, internal IP maps, or unredacted reports.

# 43. Privacy Engineering and Data Minimization

## Learning outcomes
- Identify personal and sensitive data flows.
- Minimize collection, access, retention, and exposure.
- Review logs and evidence for accidental data leakage.

## Practical exercise
Create a data inventory for a fictional learning app:
- Data element and purpose
- Collection point and storage location
- Who can access it
- Retention period
- Encryption and deletion approach
- Whether it is necessary at all

## Evidence hygiene
Use synthetic accounts and redact identifiers. Avoid uploading real logs, screenshots, customer records, API keys, tokens, or private repository content to AI tools. Apply applicable laws and organizational policy; this module is educational, not legal advice.

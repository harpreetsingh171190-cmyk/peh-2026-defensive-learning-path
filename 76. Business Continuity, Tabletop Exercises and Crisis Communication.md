# Module 76 — Business Continuity, Tabletop Exercises and Crisis Communication

## Goal
Practice decisions under pressure before a real incident.

## Tabletop format (60–90 minutes)
- **Scenario:** fictional, e.g. "a vendor notifies us that a shared credential may be exposed."
- **Injects:** new facts every 10–15 minutes (customer impact, press inquiry, regulator question).
- **Roles:** incident lead, scribe, technical lead, communications, legal/privacy, business owner.
- **Outputs:** decision log, gaps, owners, due dates.

## Crisis communication
- Say what is known, unknown, and when the next update arrives.
- Avoid speculation and blame; keep one source of truth.
- Pre-approve templates for internal, customer and regulator messages.

## Metrics
Time to detect, time to decide, time to contain, time to recover, and action-item closure rate.

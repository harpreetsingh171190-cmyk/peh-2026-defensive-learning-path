# 51. Incident Response and Evidence Handling

## Response sequence
1. **Prepare:** contacts, roles, logging, backups and playbooks.
2. **Identify:** validate alerts using reliable telemetry and note uncertainty.
3. **Contain:** follow the approved incident plan; avoid destructive improvisation.
4. **Preserve:** capture relevant timestamps, logs and system state with access controls.
5. **Eradicate and recover:** remediate the cause, restore safely and monitor.
6. **Learn:** write a blameless post-incident review with owners and due dates.

## Evidence hygiene
- Follow applicable law, organizational policy and chain-of-custody requirements.
- Record who collected an artifact, when, from which system and by what approved method.
- Preserve originals; work from copies and hash files where appropriate.
- Keep evidence encrypted with access limited to authorized responders.
- Do not upload sensitive logs or dumps to public tools or repositories.
- Avoid collecting more personal data than necessary.

## Exercise
Use the synthetic authentication log included in the course. Build a timeline, separate facts from hypotheses, identify what additional evidence would confirm each hypothesis, and draft a short incident report. Do not investigate real accounts without authorization.

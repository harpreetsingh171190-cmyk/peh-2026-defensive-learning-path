# Module 82 — Security Governance, Policy and Compliance Basics

## Goal
See how technical work connects to policy, risk ownership and audits.

## Topics
- Policies, standards, procedures and guidelines: what each is for.
- Risk ownership: the business owner accepts risk; security advises.
- Common frameworks (verify current versions): NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001, CIS Controls.
- Evidence for audits: dated, sanitized, repeatable.
- Exceptions: time-boxed, approved, reviewed.

## Lab
Draft a one-page "Acceptable Use and Lab Safety Policy" for your own home lab. Include scope, allowed activities, prohibited activities, data handling, and review date.

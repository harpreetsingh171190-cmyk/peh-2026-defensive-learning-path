# 38. Incident Response and Digital Evidence

## Lifecycle
Preparation → identification → containment → eradication → recovery → lessons learned.

## Evidence hygiene
- Preserve originals; work from copies.
- Record who collected an item, when, from where, and by what method.
- Record time zone and clock uncertainty.
- Hash files where appropriate and document the hashing method.
- Avoid unnecessary changes to affected systems.
- Follow organizational policy and legal requirements.

## Tabletop exercise
A fictional developer token appears in a public repository. Produce:
1. Timeline and initial facts.
2. Containment steps (revoke/rotate token; assess access logs).
3. Scope and impact questions.
4. Recovery and secret-scanning plan.
5. Communication draft and lessons learned.

Do not test leaked tokens or access systems using them.

# Module 79 — Security Awareness and Phishing Resilience

## Goal
Help people and organizations recognize and report social-engineering attempts. This module is defensive: no live phishing, no real targets.

## Recognition checklist
- Urgency, fear or reward used to rush a decision.
- Sender/domain mismatch, look-alike domains, unexpected attachments or login links.
- Requests for passwords, codes or payment changes outside normal process.
- Verify through a second, known channel before acting.

## Organizational controls
- One-click "report phishing" button and a no-blame reporting culture.
- Phishing-resistant MFA (passkeys / security keys), email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
- Awareness exercises only with written approval from leadership, HR and legal; never punish reporters.

## Lab
Write a one-page staff guide: "What to do in the first 5 minutes after clicking a suspicious link." Include who to call, what to preserve, and which accounts to review. Use fictional names only.

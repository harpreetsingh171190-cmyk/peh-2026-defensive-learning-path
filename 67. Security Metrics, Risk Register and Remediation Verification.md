# 67. Security Metrics, Risk Register and Remediation Verification

## Useful metrics
Prefer metrics that drive decisions over vanity counts. Examples include time to remediate by severity, recurring findings, percentage of critical assets with owners, tested backup restore rate, alert false-positive rate and percentage of fixes independently verified. Define numerator, denominator, owner, source and review cadence.

## Risk register fields
- Unique finding ID and concise title
- Asset and business owner
- Scope and date observed
- Evidence reference (not exposed secrets or unnecessary personal data)
- Impact, likelihood, severity rationale and confidence
- Recommended control, accountable owner and due date
- Status, exception expiry and residual risk
- Retest method, result and verifier

## Close-out process
1. Confirm the remediation is deployed in the intended environment.
2. Re-test only within approved scope using the smallest effective test.
3. Check for regressions and preserve concise evidence.
4. Record pass/fail and residual risk; do not mark fixed based only on a promise or configuration screenshot.
5. If the issue remains, update the risk owner and next action.

## Exercise
Create three fictional findings of different severity. Assign owners and deadlines, define a measurable remediation test, and explain how you would report overdue risk.

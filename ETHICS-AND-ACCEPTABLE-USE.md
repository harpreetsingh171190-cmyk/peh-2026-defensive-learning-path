# Ethics and Acceptable Use

This project teaches defensive security and authorized testing.

## You must
- Obtain written authorization and a defined scope before testing any system you do not own.
- Practice in isolated, disposable labs with synthetic data.
- Minimize evidence collection and protect any data you encounter.
- Stop and report immediately if you exceed scope or find unexpected sensitive data.
- Follow applicable laws, contracts and responsible-disclosure norms.

## You must not
- Use this material against systems, networks or people without permission.
- Commit secrets, personal data or real client evidence to this repository.
- Present the self-study dashboard record as a professional certification.

## Maintainer policy
Contributions that primarily enable unauthorized access, evasion of detection for criminal
purposes, or harm to third parties will not be accepted.
